# Assets: what is not in this folder, where it comes from, and its licence

Nothing in this list is stored in git. The assets are received separately as tarballs (section "Bundle and delivery" below); once unpacked into one folder they are the asset bundle (`wuji_floating_assets_20261120/` in our own build). `python code/wf.py assets verify` and `python code/wf.py verify --levels L0 --out DIR` check it against `asset_manifests/`.

## Bundle and delivery

- **Code:** the public GitHub repository https://github.com/Exiam6/wuji-floating-release (anyone can clone it; it holds no assets).
- **Assets:** tarballs, one per part (plain tar, no compression: a gzip sample of the large files saved only 7 % to 21 %), in a drive folder that the project shares (the assets do not go through GitHub): the folder `full` holds all of them with SHA256SUMS, ASSET_TERMS.txt and README.txt, and a sibling folder `rollout_only` repeats the manifest files of the CPU-only subset (its four tarballs are the same files as in `full`).
- **Sets:** the **rollout-only set** = core, hands, batches, licences, a subset of the same files (download only these four tarballs and SHA256SUMS from `full`, then `sha256sum -c --ignore-missing SHA256SUMS`); it runs the CPU rollouts (robot-hand protocols included) once the MANO file is in place, and holds no model weights; the **full set** = every part but mano and hug1 (both obtained by the user); it also runs the GPU jobs (HUG2, the 50k fine-tune, DINOv2, the pictures and the DSRL training sets; HUG1 after `wf.py assets fetch-hug1`).
- **MANO:** not delivered: every user registers at https://mano.is.tue.mpg.de, downloads the MANO models (version 1.2) and puts MANO_RIGHT.pkl at mano/models/MANO_RIGHT.pkl in the asset root; `wf.py assets verify --parts mano` checks its sha256 and says what is missing.
- **Unpacked:** all tarballs unpack into one folder, the asset bundle (wuji_floating_assets_<date>/ in our own build); its delivered files are listed in asset_manifests/SHA256SUMS.all.
- **Results back:** for an issued batch: one archive (tar of the folder `wf.py pack-results` prepares) uploaded to returns/<name>/<date>/ in the drive folder shared with you for returns, with its sha256 sent to the project.

The bundle is the 81,034 delivered files (23.23 GB) in the layout `code/wf_lib/paths.py` describes. `asset_manifests/SHA256SUMS.all` lists them (`sha256sum -c` from the asset root; add `--ignore-missing` when you hold only some parts); its sha256 is `4b3c203a38d61cd2dde4d69195365b4706b9fe8eaf1d173f3f89e3629a4ca149`. Our own build folder holds a copy of that list as `SHA256SUMS` beside `assets.json` and `ASSET_TERMS.txt`; the tarballs do not carry these three files (the release folder has them). The two expected files that are not delivered, `mano/models/MANO_RIGHT.pkl` and `ckpt/hug1/hug_full.safetensors`, are listed only in `asset_manifests/mano.sha256` and `asset_manifests/hug1.sha256`.
